## About me

I build at the intersection of **AI, developer tools, privacy and product engineering**.

I enjoy turning vague, messy ideas into usable systems. Especially where **LLMs, agents, benchmarking, automation and real product constraints** meet.

My interests currently include:
- AI agents and agentic workflows
- privacy engineering and evaluation
- LLM benchmarking and prompt design
- developer infrastructure and applied ML
- product thinking and fast execution

## Selected work

### Derbent
Privacy-focused infrastructure and evaluation for AI applications.  
Built around privacy transformation, policy-aware redaction, model benchmarking and reliable handling of sensitive data.  
The project focuses on making privacy systems measurable, testable and practical for real AI workflows.

### Needle
An AI-powered email and workday assistant for managing inboxes, follow-ups, scheduling, drafting and commitments.  
Built with approval-first workflows, relationship memory, pre-send risk checks and integrations across email, calendar, CRM and files.  
Deployed as a full-stack product with FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker and background workers.

### Unicore
A social product that reached **2,000+ installs**.  
I worked across product development, launch and real user feedback rather than treating it as a purely technical project.  
It gave me hands-on experience with turning an idea into a product people actually used.

## Current focus

I’m currently exploring the engineering and evaluation of **reliable AI systems**, with a particular interest in:

- **LLM evaluation and benchmark design** - measuring capability, failure modes, robustness and cost
- **Privacy-preserving AI** - policy-aware transformation, sensitive-data handling and trust boundaries
- **Agent reliability** - verification, tool use, memory and failure-resistant workflows
- **Human-in-the-loop systems** - designing AI that knows when to act, ask, verify or defer
- **Applied AI infrastructure** - turning research ideas into systems that can be tested, deployed and observed
